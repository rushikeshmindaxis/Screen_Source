Material Performance

Technical Key Features

Engineered on a premium biaxially oriented PET film base for exceptional physical and thermal durability.

100% Waterproof & Tear-Proof

Unmatched mechanical strength that withstands severe tearing forces, moisture, rain, and harsh industrial environments.

  • Zero Moisture Absorption: Film does not expand, warp, or degrade when submerged in water or oil.
  • High Tensile Strength: Tear-resistant PET core preserves graphic integrity under repeated flexure.
  • Chemical Resistance: Impervious to mild acids, industrial solvents, grease, and cleaning chemicals.

Multi-Process Printability

Formulated with a proprietary double-sided matte coating for seamless ink and toner anchorage across print technologies.

  • Digital Electrophotography: Sharp toner transfer without static cling or jamming in digital production presses.
  • Offset & Flexo Printing: Instant ink setting with quick drying and high color density output.
  • Screen Printing: Exceptional opacity and sharp detail reproduction for UV and solvent screen inks.

Thermal & Dimensional Stability

Maintains absolute flatness and tight dimensional tolerances across high-temperature digital fusing cycles.

  • Heat Resistant Base: Resists thermal deformation during high-speed laser printer fusing.
  • Anti-Static Coating: Eliminates static charge buildup for smooth sheet feeding in automated sorters.
  • Recyclable PET Core: Eco-friendly polyester base suitable for industrial recycling programs.
