Screen Exposing Machines

Controlled UV screen exposing equipment transfers high-density positive artwork onto photo-sensitive emulsion coated screens with sub-micron edge definition.

Equipped with vacuum drawdown systems for intimate film-to-emulsion contact during UV exposure cycles.

Key Features:

  • High-vacuum drawdown system ensures crisp film-to-mesh contact.
  • Sharp stencil resolution for fine lines, dots, and half-tones.
  • Programmable exposure timer for repeatable emulsion hardening.
  • Suitable for solvent-resistant, UV, and water-based emulsion systems.

Screen Stretching Machines

Achieve perfect, uniform tension every time with pneumatic screen stretching machinery designed to apply controlled tension across all screen sizes.

Eliminates slow manual hand-stretching for faster setup, perfectly taut screens, and consistent registration accuracy.

Key Features:

  • Uniform pneumatic pressure eliminates mesh wrinkles & loose spots.
  • Rapid stretching cycle reduces setup time significantly.
  • Adjustable pneumatic clamps accommodate various aluminum and wooden frames.
  • Heavy-duty steel construction for reliable long-term production.
