Workflow

Our Screen Exposing Process

A structured 5-step prepress workflow engineered for maximum accuracy.

STEP 01

Artwork Preparation

Review and prepare the printing artwork and positive film for optical density.

STEP 02

Screen Preparation

Ensure the emulsion-coated screen is clean, dry, and properly degreased.

STEP 03

UV Exposure

Expose the screen using controlled UV exposure units under optimal vacuum drawdown.

STEP 04

Washing & Development

Wash out unexposed emulsion with precision water spray to reveal the required stencil.

STEP 05

Inspection & Finishing

Inspect the stencil for edge definition, pinholes, and overall finish before delivery.

Technical Synergies

The Right Combination of Mesh, Emulsion & Exposure

Screen performance depends on selecting the right combination of mesh, emulsion and exposure parameters for the application.

Mesh

Selection based on application requirements, ink system, line count, and desired deposit thickness.

Emulsion

Selection based on ink chemistry (solvent, UV, water-based), stencil durability, and RZ roughness targets.

Exposure

Controlled UV exposure timing to achieve optimal cross-linking, sharp edges, and maximum stencil life.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our professional screen exposing services.

What is screen exposing?
Screen exposing transfers the required artwork onto the photo-sensitive emulsion coated on a printing screen using UV light.
Why is correct exposure important?
Correct exposure helps achieve good stencil definition, durability and consistent printing performance across long print runs.
Can you expose screens for industrial printing?
Yes. Screensource provides screen exposing services for industrial and specialty printing applications including membrane switches, automotive, and electronics.
Can you help with emulsion selection?
Yes. We can recommend suitable emulsion options based on the mesh count, ink system and specific application requirements.
Do you provide screen stretching and exposing together?
Yes. Screensource can provide both screen stretching and exposing services as part of an integrated pre-press process.
