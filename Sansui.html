Sunsui Tapes

Sunsui Tapes is a well-established brand offering a comprehensive range of industrial-grade adhesive tapes. Designed for demanding applications in printing, packaging, and manufacturing, Sunsui is especially recognized for its specialized double-sided tapes that provide strong, reliable, and versatile bonding solutions.

Key Features & Applications

  • Double-Sided Tissue Tapes: Non-woven tissue carrier coated with powerful adhesive for clean, discreet, high-tack bonding. Ideal for:
    • Screen printing – mounting screens, securing substrates
    • Foam & felt lamination in automotive/appliance manufacturing
    • Paper, film, foil splicing in packaging & printing
    • Craft & DIY – posters, photos, decorative elements
  • Repulpable Splicing Tapes: Water-soluble adhesive for the paper/printing industry, fully recyclable with paper – ideal for high-speed splicing in newsprint and paper processing.
  • High-Performance Polyester Tapes: Heat-resistant adhesives for:
    • High-temperature masking & bonding
    • Laminating decorative profiles, moldings, trims
    • Heavy-duty bonding to metal, plastic, powder-coated surfaces

Sunsui tapes are engineered for reliability and performance, making them a trusted choice for professionals needing consistent results and high-quality materials.
