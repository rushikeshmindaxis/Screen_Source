Technical Specifications

TechNova Product Range

Engineered thermal and digital printing substrates delivering superior ink toner adhesion, dimensional stability, and archival longevity.

Non-Tear Synthetic Papers

NovaLife Matte PET double-sided synthetic papers (115, 125, 175, 200 DS) built on durable biaxially oriented PET film base.

  • 100% Waterproof & Tear-Proof: Impervious to water, grease, chemical spills, and high physical strain.
  • Both-Side Matte Coating: Ensures sharp print contrast, instant toner anchorage, and smudge-free handling.
  • Thermal Stability: High heat resistance prevents film curling during laser toner fusing cycles.

Backlit & Translucent PET

NovaLife Clarus & Backlit PET films (110, 125, 190 SS) formulated for uniform light diffusion in backlit lightboxes.

  • High Light Translucency: Diffuses LED backlighting evenly without hot spots or visual graininess.
  • Vibrant Color Saturation: Micro-porous coating absorbs eco-solvent, UV, and latex inks for rich color depth.
  • Dimensional Flatness: Stiff PET base remains flat without buckling under hot light box fixtures.

Specialty Metallic & Art Papers

NovaLife Silver Metallic PET & Gloss Art Papers (170, 200 DS) for premium photo albums, luxury packaging, and certificates.

  • Metallic Silver Effect: Eye-catching reflective metallic surface adds a regal touch to luxury prints.
  • Gloss Art Finish: Mirror-smooth gloss surface reproduces fine photographic details and crisp typography.
  • Archival Durability: Acid-free formulation preserves graphics against yellowing and atmospheric aging.
