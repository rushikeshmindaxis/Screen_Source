Technical Specifications

POLYVANTIS Product Range

Premium thermoplastic polycarbonate films engineered for high transparency, impact resistance, and thermoforming durability.

8010 Clear Film

Polished double-sided optical polycarbonate film providing exceptional clarity, high light transmission, and dimensional heat stability.

  • High Optical Clarity: Crystal clear double-polished finish ideal for LED displays, touchscreens, and illuminated dials.
  • Printability & Formability: Compatible with solvent, UV screen inks, and deep thermoforming drawing operations.
  • Heat & Flame Resistance: High glass transition temperature withstands harsh processing and elevated ambient temperatures.

8B35 Velvet Matte Film

One-side velvet, one-side fine matte film designed to eliminate glare while shielding printed graphics from finger abrasion.

  • Anti-Glare Texture: Matte surface diffuses light reflection for crisp graphic readability in bright environments.
  • Tactile Feedback: Ideal for membrane keypads, control panels, and automotive dashboard instrumentation.
  • Scratch & Chemical Resistance: Resists surface scuffing, household cleaner breakdown, and solvent exposure.

Key Advantages & Applications

POLYVANTIS polycarbonate films deliver proven performance across high-reliability industrial and electronics applications.

  • Die-Cutting Precision: Excellent gauge uniformity ensures clean die-cutting, embossing, and laser processing.
  • Industrial Applications: Automotive dials, medical equipment overlays, home appliances, and backlit signage.
  • Legacy Performance: Formerly manufactured under the SABIC Innovative Plastics portfolio with identical certified specs.
