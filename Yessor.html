Yessor Product Range

Comprehensive critical cleaning wipe solutions manufactured under ISO-certified processes for zero particle contamination.

100% Lint-Free Cleanroom Wipes

Constructed from 100% continuous filament polyester yarn to eliminate fiber shedding in controlled ISO Class 4-8 environments.

  • Zero Particle Shedding: Knitted double-knit structure with sealed edges prevents loose fibers from contaminating sensitive screens.
  • Laser-Cut Sealed Edges: Ultrasonic edge sealing traps loose threads to protect delicate electronic and optical surfaces.
  • Cleanroom Packaged: Processed and double-vacuum packed in Class 100 cleanrooms for maximum purity.

High Solvent & Spill Absorbency

Exceptional fluid retention for efficient stencil washup, solvent wiping, and heavy-duty spill cleanup.

  • High Retention Capacity: Holds multi-fold liquid volume for fast wiping cycles and reduced wipe consumption.
  • Tear-Resistant Durability: High tensile strength withstands vigorous scrubbing across rough aluminum frames and screen meshes.
  • Solvent Compatibility: Resistant to press washes, alcohols, cyclohexanone, acetone, and cleaning solvents.

Certified Safety & Applications

Trusted across demanding industrial, pharmaceutical, aerospace, and screen printing applications nationwide.

  • Regulatory Compliance: Meets USFDA, MHRA, and GMP standards; nitrosamine-free and food-grade certified.
  • Screen Printing Prepress: Essential for screen cleaning, stencil degreasing, and ink duct maintenance.
  • High Precision Optics & Electronics: Safe for cleaning glass lenses, PCB assemblies, and semiconductor tools.
